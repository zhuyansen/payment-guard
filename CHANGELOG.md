# Changelog

## 0.4.3 (2026-11-21)

- Lemon Squeezy: the warning now says what moving to Stripe Managed Payments involves. The promotional 1.5% (through 1 December 2027) comes on top of Stripe's standard payment processing fees; subscriptions paid with PayPal can't migrate; Managed Payments doesn't deliver license keys or files and doesn't include affiliates; it takes only businesses based in Stripe's supported locations (Hong Kong, the UK and the US among them, not mainland China), and buyers in China can't purchase through it. Sources: [Lemon Squeezy migration guide](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/migration), [Managed Payments eligibility](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/managed-payments/eligibility).
- PayPal, mainland China individual: the line on paypal.com personal accounts opened after 30 June 2026 now links PayPal's own text, the Chinese Mainland section of the [PayPal user agreement](https://www.paypal.com/c2/legalhub/paypal/useragreement-full) (notice issued 28 May 2026, in force 30 June 2026), and says what it says: such accounts can't take payments as a seller, goods and services included, only personal payments from friends and family, and can't be converted to business accounts. The PayPal.cn seller account and its steps are unchanged.
- Data checked 2026-11-21 for these two entries. The account-opening steps (read 2026-10-17), review lists and category stances (2026-10-16) were not re-read.
